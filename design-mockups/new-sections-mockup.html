Portfolio Card — updated

How the existing project cards change: a third button that jumps to the demo and starts it.

Behaviour: clicking Watch Demo scrolls to that project's card in the Demos section and starts the video — one click, not two. The video itself lives only in the Demos section, so nothing is duplicated. Try the buttons below — the target card flashes and shows a "Playing" state.

Real implementation: the thumbnail stays a static image until clicked, then swaps to <iframe src="https://www.youtube.com/embed/VIDEO_ID?autoplay=1">. That keeps the page fast (no YouTube script loads until someone actually wants the video) and satisfies browser autoplay rules, since the play is triggered by a real user click.
